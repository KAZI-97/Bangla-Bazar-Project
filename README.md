<h1 className="text-4xl font-extrabold text-green-500">Project name -  Bangla Bazar </h1> 
<h2 className="font-semibold">Short description About the Project</h2>
<p>Bangla Bazar is an online platform just like Your nearest local Market. From here you can buy your daily grocery with lesser market price. You see the product depend on different categories and will be able to find your required product easily. Beside, you can see the change of product price like which product price goes up and goes down. The most important thing of this bangla bazar project is you can see the price of your product in different market around Bangladesh. You will be able to see the product but if u want to see the details of that product, You must have to Create an Account or if you already created an account, you must have to sign in.</p>

<h3>5 key features of the project </h3>
<ul>
<li>There is different Category of Product and when you click on a Category, it will route You to another Page where you Will be Able to See Different Product Under that Category with Price</li>
<li>In different product under one Category, you will be able to sort them in both ascending and descending order </li>
<li>Without Sign In or Sign Up you will not be able to see the product details or Category Wise Product</li>
<li>In Profile Page there is a update profile button which will help you to update your name</li>
<li>By clicking any product cart, we will be able to see the details of that specific product and their market price depend on different market around Bangladesh</li>
</ul>
